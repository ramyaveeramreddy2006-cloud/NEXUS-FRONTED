# NEXUS | Company project brief

## Positioning

**NEXUS is a multi-domain opportunity and service platform that connects people and organizations with relevant resources, opportunities, experts and communities, while making cross-field collaboration possible through one identity.**

NEXUS is not positioned as the first platform to combine content and opportunities. Its product thesis is that a shared identity, field-based personalization and cross-domain discovery can connect people whose needs span more than one field.

## Problem and product response

People often need information and collaborators beyond the service or industry where they began looking. A grower may need an agriculture expert, a software builder and a funding partner to solve one farm problem. A student may need a course, a mentor, a project and an employer connection. NEXUS gives each person one profile and a relevant view across selected fields, then makes adjacent-field resources and people discoverable.

| User need | NEXUS response |
|---|---|
| Too much unrelated information | Select fields and receive a personalized dashboard |
| Separate accounts and communities | Keep one identity while following multiple fields |
| A solution requires people from other fields | Surface cross-domain resources, opportunities and collaborations |
| Organizations need to reach relevant people | Provide organization accounts and tools to publish and manage listings |

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in the ecosystem | Primary needs |
|---|---|---|
| End users: students, farmers, professionals, job seekers and citizens | Discover and participate | Relevant opportunities, useful services, expert access and trusted communities |
| Organizations: companies, startups, institutions, NGOs and public-service groups | Supply opportunities and services | A profile, publishing tools, relevant reach and listing management |
| Experts: faculty, mentors and domain practitioners | Supply knowledge and guidance | A discoverable profile, useful resources and collaboration requests |
| Platform administrators | Protect ecosystem quality | Organization review, moderation, reporting and platform analytics |

## Differentiator and example journey

The differentiator to test is **cross-domain collaboration around a concrete need**. Example: a farmer identifies an irrigation challenge, discovers an agriculture resource and an expert, then finds a technology collaborator to prototype a solution and a business organization to support deployment. Each participant uses one account; each sees information relevant to their fields.

This should be demonstrated as a complete user journey, not presented as a claim that NEXUS has already achieved marketplace scale or verified outcomes.

## Initial domain focus

Build one reusable platform and demonstrate three journeys deeply:

1. **Education:** courses and learning resources, scholarships and internships, project opportunities, mentors and events.
2. **Agriculture:** practical resources, agricultural experts, public programmes, farm-related projects and technology collaborators.
3. **Business and technology:** jobs, internships, organization listings, project teams and connections to needs in other fields.

Healthcare, government and community remain selectable fields on the shared platform. Expand their specialist workflows when user research supports them.

## Final feature set

### Core user platform

- Secure account and a single profile with multiple selected fields.
- Personalized dashboard and recommendations based on those fields.
- Search and filters for resources, opportunities, events and community posts.
- Cross-domain discovery to connect a need with people and resources in adjacent fields.
- Multilingual interface, AI-assisted discovery and accessible voice interaction.
- Saved resources and relevant community discussions.

### Organization workspace

- Organization account and profile, with verification status.
- Create and manage jobs, internships, events, resources and programmes.
- Find relevant users and experts by field and profile.
- Track listing status and incoming expressions of interest.

### Trust and administration

- Review organization identities and submitted listings.
- Moderate posts and handle user reports.
- Review engagement and listing quality through platform analytics.
- Apply ownership and role checks to all write and delete operations.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Evaluation-ready core:** multi-field profiles, personalized dashboard, domain-filtered search and organization-owned listings.
2. **Trustworthy marketplace:** organization verification, listing approval, expressions of interest and reporting.
3. **Cross-domain workflows:** expert discovery, collaboration requests, team formation and outcome tracking.
4. **Scale and accessibility:** richer localized content, assistive voice features, analytics and deployment hardening.

## Company presentation

> “NEXUS is a shared opportunity and service platform for people and organizations whose needs cross industry boundaries. Users keep one profile, select the fields relevant to them, and discover resources, opportunities and collaborators across those fields. Our first product test is whether personalized cross-domain discovery helps people form useful collaborations—for example, connecting an agriculture need with an expert, a technology builder and a supporting organization.”

## Evaluation measures

- Percentage of users who select more than one field.
- Search-to-action rate for resources and opportunities.
- Cross-domain views, introductions and collaboration requests.
- Organization listing completion and approval time.
- Return use and user-rated relevance of recommendations.
